AI & Future of Work — Student Feature Guide

futureofjobs.vercel.app · Data: BLS OES, O*NET 28.2, Anthropic Claude
This guide explains every feature of the AI & Future of Work visualisation tool. Each feature is designed to help you analyse how artificial intelligence is reshaping specific job roles — so you can make strategic decisions about your career or studies.
01🏔️

3D Terrain Map

Each glowing peak represents one job role. Peak height = number of people employed in that role. Peak colour = AI automation risk: red means high risk, green means the role is largely safe from automation.

Student tip: Taller peaks mean more jobs exist today — watch them shrink as you move the time slider forward.
02⏱️

AI Timeline Slider

Drag the slider from 2025 → 2030 to simulate how AI displaces workers over time. Peaks fall faster for high-risk roles. The animation shows where the biggest structural changes are expected.

Student tip: Compare a Marketing Manager to a Sales Manager at 2030 — notice which peak drops faster and by how much.
03📋

Job Detail Panel

Click any peak to open a role's full profile: automation risk %, growth outlook to 2032, a list of its most human-critical tasks, and a BLS employment trend sparkline.

Student tip: The "Human Skills" section shows what AI cannot easily replace. These are the tasks worth developing.
04🤖

AI Analysis (Analyze)

Tap the Analyze button on any role. Claude AI reads the O*NET task list and scores each task individually for AI replaceability vs. human criticality — in real time, not from a pre-written database.

Student tip: Every score is freshly generated. The AI reasons about each specific task, so results reflect genuine capability assessment.
05🔮

Scenario Planning

The Scenario button asks "what if" — what happens to this role if AI adoption accelerates, if regulation tightens, or if economic conditions shift? It generates a written strategic brief.

Student tip: Use this to stress-test career choices. Ask the same role under two different scenarios and compare the outlooks.
06🗺️

Career Roadmap

Identifies the highest-risk task in a role and builds a personalised 6-month upskilling plan to move away from it — covering specific skills, tools, and learning resources.

Student tip: Available from the Job Detail Panel after running an AI Analysis. The plan is tailored to that specific role's risk profile.
07🎯

My Skills (CV Match)

Paste your CV or a list of your skills. The app analyses which roles align with your strengths and flags where you are most exposed to automation risk.

Student tip: Honest input = honest output. List the skills you actually have, not the ones you wish you had.
08🗺️

US Map View

Switch from the 3D terrain to a flat 2D US map. Circle size shows employment concentration by state — so you can see whether automation risk is evenly distributed or regionally concentrated.

Student tip: Toggle using the "Map" button in the top navigation. Useful for understanding local labour market exposure.
09📊

Job Security Index

The colour legend on the right of the screen maps roles into three bands: High Automation Risk (red), Hybrid/Augmented (amber), and Safe Human-Centric (green). Bands are relative — graded on a curve across all 50 roles shown.

Student tip: A "green" role here means it is safer than most others in this dataset — not that it is completely immune to automation.
10📚

Data Sources

Employment numbers come from the Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics. Task descriptions are sourced from O*NET 28.2. AI risk scores are generated live by Anthropic Claude.

Student tip: Click "Methodology & Data" at the bottom of the screen to read the full sourcing and calculation methodology.
